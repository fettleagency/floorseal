Powerful, targeted black spot elimination for patios and paving

Anyone who has tried to remove black spot lichen from a patio or flagstones will know how difficult it is. Black spot does not respond to pressure washing alone because it is deeply ingrained, and bleach simply doesn’t work. Black Spot Remover Plus is made for natural stone, block pavers and concrete patios.

It is significantly stronger than normal patio cleaners and other black spot removers, and is an excellent choice for cleaning Indian sandstones, Yorkshire sandstone flags, block pavers and concrete. As well as black spot lichen it removes algae, bird droppings, mould, dirt and grime.

It is easy to use: apply and allow to dwell, keep the surface wet with product, then rinse off. It can be used without the need for a pressure washer.

How to use

Please refer to the Technical Data Sheet for full instructions. Wear appropriate PPE, safety goggles & gloves. Wear suitable overalls or old clothing as this product can discolour natural fibres. Carry out a test in an inconspicuous area. If you find this product is not suitable for use on your project, do not continue to use.

  1. 01

    Lightly pre-wet the area to be treated with clean water & the edges of adjacent lawns/plants beds (which may encounter the solution). Do not spray onto adjacent plants & lawns or allow the product to mist over them. Do not allow this product to enter ponds or encounter aquatic life.

  2. 02

    Apply undiluted with a sprayer, watering can or direct from the container. Apply in smaller manageable sections when treating large areas. Do not exceed the coverage rates. Please allow for variation in coverage due to porosity & level of infestation.

  3. 03

    Keep the surface wet by applying more product so the surface is always visibly wet. Lightly agitate with a stiff yard brush. Extend the dwell time for as long as necessary, up to 1 hour +. Do not allow the product to dry on the surface or it ceases to work! Add more product during the dwell time to keep the surface visibly saturated/wet.

  4. 04

    Rinse away with plenty of clean water and allow to dry. Repeat as required.

This product can be successfully applied in damp or dry weather conditions. Safe for children, pets & wildlife when dry. Follow up with Floorseal Green Remover Concentrate once or twice per year to maintain the surface and inhibit the return of black spot lichens.
