More about Indian sandstone cleaning and sealing

Looking for the best patio cleaning products?

Floorseal has the best range of patio cleaners to suit all your patio cleaning requirements. For general and deep cleaning of your patio we recommend Floorseal Patio Cleaner Plus. For additional cleaning power and the removal of ingrained black spot use Floorseal Black Spot Remover Plus.

For general annual maintenance of any type of patio, particularly the removal of algae or any green organic matter, use Floorseal Green Remover Concentrate. It can be sprayed on and left to work over days, weeks or months, and keeps working for up to a year after application.

What’s the best way to seal a patio?

There are various types of sealer for different types of patio. Natural Stone Sealer Breathe retains an unaltered natural appearance on sandstones and porous stone. Indian Sandstone Sealer & Colour Intensifier seals and enhances naturally colourful sandstones. Porcelain Tile Sealer for Patios applies a protective top coat to modern porcelain patios.

We have a wide range of patio sealers in our sealers section to choose from.
